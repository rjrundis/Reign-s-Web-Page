    <h2>Why I Chose Kesha</h2>

    <p>
      Kesha is my favorite artist because ever since
      I was a little girl, I have always loved her
      music. She gives me the diva vibes I need to
      keep moving forward through everything.
      Her songs can be energetic and fun, but she
      also has music that is emotional and meaningful.
      I like artists who are not afraid to try
      different sounds, and Kesha has done that
      throughout her career. Her music reminds me
      that there is more than one way to express
      yourself.
    </p>

    <h2>Her Music and Career</h2>

    <p>
      Kesha became widely known for catchy pop
      songs like "TiK ToK" and "Your Love Is My Drug."
      Over time, she released music with different
      moods and styles. Albums such as Animal,
      Warrior, Rainbow, and Gag Order show how
      her sound has changed. What stands out to
      me is how she balances upbeat music with
      songs that tell a more personal story.
      Listening to different parts of her career
      shows how an artist can grow without
      losing her identity.
    </p>

    <h2>Why Music Matters to Me</h2>

    <p>
      I have spent a lot of time around music
      because I played the violin, so I appreciate
      how music can affect people's emotions.
      I enjoy listening to different genres,
      and Kesha is one artist I keep coming back to.
      Sometimes I want something upbeat, and
      other times I want music that feels meaningful.
      I like that music gives people freedom
      to be themselves. That is one reason
      I wanted to make my first website
      about an artist I enjoy.
    </p>
